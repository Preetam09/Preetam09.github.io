Pedestrian trajectory prediction for robot navigation

In progress
CSCE 689 Multi-Robot Systems · Sep 2026 – present

A graph-transformer model that predicts where pedestrians will walk, so a robot can plan a safe path through a crowd. I benchmark prediction error (ADE/FDE) on the ETH/UCY datasets, keep the robot at a safe distance with control barrier functions, and test it on the Robotarium robot testbed, where I also measure how much it costs the robot to finish its task. Results will be added here when the project finishes. The demo on the home page is a much simpler version of the idea.
