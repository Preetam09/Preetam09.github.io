Robots in crowds

A robot in a crowd has to guess where people are about to walk, not just where they are now. My course project trains a graph-transformer model to make that guess. This demo is a much simpler version: it predicts each person's next 1.6 seconds with a straight line and steers around those predictions. Click anywhere to give the robot a new goal.
